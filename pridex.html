Overview

PriDex combines video-based 3D reconstruction with a confidence-guided RL teacher to generate physically validated robot demonstrations. Trained teachers can repeatedly roll out successful task executions, producing data for downstream visual policy learning.

Egocentric video → 3D reconstruction → Confidence-guided RL teacher → Successful rollouts → Robot demonstrations → Visual policy deployment

Learning from Uncertain Reconstruction

Human videos provide useful motion and contact references. Reconstructed geometry can contain hand–object gaps, penetration, and tracking drift, while robot kinematics impose additional constraints. PriDex uses reconstruction confidence to adapt how closely the RL teacher follows those references.

Method

01 / RECONSTRUCT

Video Reconstruction

Reconstruct hand and object motion from egocentric video to obtain trajectories and contact cues. Spatial and temporal alignment, grasp filtering, and inverse kinematics adapt these references to the robot.

02 / ESTIMATE CONFIDENCE

Frame-Level Reconstruction Confidence

Use 3D-to-2D reprojection and point tracking to assess object position and rotation estimates. The lowest score within each frame determines its high, medium, or low confidence category.

03 / LEARN

Confidence-Guided Residual PPO

Frame-level confidence controls the policy’s exploration range and residual action bounds. Lower confidence allows larger deviations from the reconstructed reference. A human wrist-motion direction reward provides additional guidance under low confidence.

04 / RECORD

Physically Validated Robot Data

Roll out trained RL teachers and retain executions that pass task-specific simulation success checkers. Record robot proprioception, actions, and observations from two wrist cameras and a head camera for downstream visual policy learning.
