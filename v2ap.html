V2AP

Learning Affordance Posteriors for Robotic Grasping from Human Video Priors

Yanghong Liu, Fan Xu, Zehao Lin, Jiakai Chen, Haoran Lu,
Zhengtao Yao, Yubiao Zhang, Masayoshi Tomizuka, Wei Zhan, Ruihai Wu

UC Berkeley — Tomizuka MSC Lab

Turning human video contact priors into physically verified supervision for robot grasping.

Workshop PDF ↗Results ↓Back to homepage ↗

Overview

V2AP reconstructs hand–object interactions from egocentric and third-person videos, extracts human contact priors, and verifies candidate grasps in simulation. Successful grasps form Robot Posteriors that supervise affordance learning and guide grasp-pose generation.

Method

01 / RECONSTRUCT

Human Contact Priors

Reconstruct hands and objects from egocentric and third-person videos, then align them in a shared coordinate frame. Aggregate contact evidence on the object surface and smooth it into a continuous map of human-preferred contact regions.

02 / VERIFY

Physics-Verified Robot Posteriors

Sample grasps in human-prior regions. Use cuRobo to check reachability and collision-free motion, then Isaac Sim to verify stable grasping and lifting. Contacts from successful grasps form posterior supervision.

03 / LEARN

Posterior-Supervised Affordance Learning

Train a PointNet++ network with the simulation-generated posterior maps. Given an object point cloud, the model predicts a dense map of robot-executable grasp affordances using point-wise L1 supervision.

04 / GENERATE

Affordance-Conditioned Grasp Generation

A conditional diffusion model uses the point cloud and predicted affordance map to generate 6-DoF grasp poses. Geometric, inverse-kinematics, and collision checks select a feasible grasp for execution.

Scope & Resources

The current framework focuses on single-object grasping. Extending posterior construction to bimanual, multi-object, and long-horizon manipulation will require handling more complex occlusion, contact dynamics, and coordination.
