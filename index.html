01 / ABOUT

Human demonstrations.
Robot behavior.

I’m a Statistics undergraduate at UC Berkeley with a minor in Data Science, expecting to graduate in December 2026.

At the Tomizuka MSC Lab, supervised by Ruihai Wu, I study bimanual dexterous manipulation. I’m interested in how imperfect visual observations can become useful references for robots, and how those references can support physically executable robot behavior.

My work connects reconstruction confidence and grasp selection with residual reinforcement learning and deployment on real robots.
