V2AP: Learning Affordance Posteriors for Robotic Grasping from Human Video Priors

Yanghong Liu, Fan Xu, Zehao Lin, Jiakai Chen, Haoran Lu, Zhengtao Yao, Yubiao Zhang, Masayoshi Tomizuka, Wei Zhan, Ruihai Wu

RSS 2026 RoboData Workshop · First author

Project page/Workshop paper

Turning human contact priors into robot-executable grasp affordances through physics-based validation. I led video-to-3D reconstruction and affordance-model training.

Simulated grasp success: 31.9% → 61.6% over human-prior supervision, aggregated across all test trials on unseen objects and categories.

Method overview

The pipeline reconstructs hand–object interactions from egocentric and third-person videos and extracts object-centric contact priors. Successful simulated grasps within these regions provide posterior labels for affordance learning. A learned affordance model then guides a conditional diffusion model to generate grasp poses.
